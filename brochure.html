// Installation Best Practices
  • • Cable Routing: Run sensor lines in dedicated shielded conduit separated from high-voltage motor or inverter lines by at least 150 mm.
  • • Shield Grounding: Connect overall foil shield to Earth terminal pin 03 at controller end only to eliminate ground noise loops.
  • • Transducer Focus: Center edge of substrate at 50% sensor slot depth for symmetrical left/right proportional band.
NOTICE: Standard system matches EN 60204-1 machine safety compliance. Limit switches must remain wired in closed circuit.
