// Installation Best Practices
  • • Conduit Separation: Route sensor lines in dedicated grounded conduits separated from motor inverter leads by >150 mm.
  • • Ground Shielding: Connect overall foil shield to Earth terminal pin 03 at controller end only to prevent ground loop hum.
  • • Transducer Placement: Mount sensor 50–100 mm downstream of guide roller for tightest proportional responsiveness.
